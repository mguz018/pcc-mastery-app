Privacy Policy

Top Bins Soccer · Last updated 22 August 2026

The short version

Top Bins Soccer does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

What the app stores

The game saves your progress on your device only: best score, coins and career statistics; your Match Energy and when it last regenerated; your audio and haptics settings; and which cosmetic items you have unlocked.

This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in, or registration
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No third-party crash reporting
  • No access to location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data

Purchases

Purchases of Match Energy or Unlimited Match Energy are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether a purchase succeeded, so it can grant what you bought. Apple's privacy policy covers that transaction.

Network use

The game is fully playable offline. It contacts the network only to reach Apple's App Store when you open the store screen or restore a purchase.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children. It contains in-app purchases, which can be disabled in iOS Settings under Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions.
