Common questions

Why do I have to wait to play?
Match Energy is the game's lives system. New players start with 8. Each run costs 1, one refills automatically every 10 minutes, and a run that ends in the first second or two is refunded. You never have to pay to keep playing.

My energy did not come back.
Energy regenerates from your device's clock, even while the app is closed. If your device's date and time are set manually, switch them back to automatic in Settings → General → Date & Time.

I bought Unlimited Match Energy on another device.
Open the game, tap Store on the main menu, and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID.

I was charged but did not receive my energy.
Reopen the app while connected to the internet — pending purchases are granted automatically. If it still has not arrived, email us with the date of purchase.

How do I turn off sound or haptics?
Main menu → Settings.

How do I request a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us: reportaproblem.apple.com.
